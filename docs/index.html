Taita Hills · Kenya

From forest records to climate maps

Local occurrence and climatic suitability of Coffea fadenii and Afrocarpus usambarensis.

Forest inventories from Ngangao and Mbololo describe local vegetation and focal-species occurrence. Kenya-wide Maxent models identify areas to consider for restoration assessment and further surveys.

Explore the findings ↓

Methods

Two scales of analysis

Forest inventories

Across 23 survey records, we compared woody assemblages, vegetation structure and taxon dominance, focal-stem abundance and occurrence, and the co-occurrence of juveniles and listed larger stems.

Ngangao · 9 records
Mbololo · 14 records

Climatic suitability

Maxent models compared narrow and broad calibration domains and tested occurrence thinning. The shortlist retained cells classified as suitable in every retained scenario and within all predictors’ training ranges in every fit.

Primary threshold · 10th percentile of training-presence scores
